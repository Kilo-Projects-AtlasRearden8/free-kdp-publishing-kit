# Free KDP Publishing Kit

Free printable forms for self-publishing on Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP).
Fill them out in your browser, print them, and use them to get your book launch
ready — no account, no signup, and nothing to install.

This kit was put together by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October
2026), the beginner-friendly AI education and publishing label of Dylan Asdale,
registered nurse turned AI author of *The Massive AI Manual*. Learn more at
[dylanasdale.github.io](https://dylanasdale.github.io).

Everything here is written plain English, step by step, never condescending.
Large-print friendly and designed to print cleanly from any browser.

## What's inside

| File | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| `kit/pre-launch-checklist.md` | Tasks to finish before your book goes live on KDP |
| `kit/metadata-worksheet.md` | Title, subtitle, description, categories, and keywords |
| `kit/upload-checklist.md` | Step-by-step steps for uploading your manuscript and cover |
| `kit/launch-week-calendar.md` | A day-by-day plan for launch week |

Each Markdown file has a matching printable HTML file in `html/`. The HTML
versions are fillable: click into the fields, type your answers, then print.

## How to use

1. Open any `html/*.html` file in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox,
   Safari all work).
2. Fill in the fields that apply to your book.
3. Choose **File > Print** (or press Ctrl+P / Cmd+P). The print styles hide
   on-screen controls and fit each page cleanly.
4. Save a filled copy: in the print dialog, choose **"Save as PDF"** if you
   want a digital record.

No internet connection is needed after you download the kit. Nothing is saved
to a server — your information stays on your computer.

## Large-print friendly

The HTML pages use at least 18 pt body text, high contrast black-on-white,
and roomy checkboxes so they're easy to read and fill in by hand. Print
styles strip colors and backgrounds to save ink.
