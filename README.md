# Free KDP Publishing Kit

Free printable forms for self-publishing on Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP).
Fill them out in your browser, print them, and use them to get your book launch
ready — no account, no signup, and nothing to install.

This kit was put together by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** (Indiana, formed October
2026), the beginner-friendly publishing label of Dylan Asdale — a registered
nurse turned AI author of *The Massive AI Manual*. Dylan explains things
"like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending. Learn
more at [dylanasdale.github.io](https://dylanasdale.github.io).

Everything here is written for beginners, designed to print cleanly from any
browser, and friendly to low-energy and large-print needs.

## Who this is for

Anyone publishing a book on KDP who wants a clear, low-pressure way to get
organized before launch. Especially useful if you're new to self-publishing,
prefer working on paper, or just want everything in one place before you start
clicking through Amazon's forms.

## What's inside

| File | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| `kit/pre-launch-checklist.md` | Tasks to finish before your book goes live on KDP |
| `kit/metadata-worksheet.md` | Title, subtitle, description, categories, and keywords |
| `kit/upload-checklist.md` | Step-by-step steps for uploading your manuscript and cover |
| `kit/launch-week-calendar.md` | A day-by-day plan for launch week |

Each Markdown file has a matching **fillable HTML page** in `html/`. Click into
the fields, type your answers, then print — or choose **"Save as PDF"** in the
print dialog to keep a digital copy of your filled-in form.

## How to use

1. Open any `html/*.html` file in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox,
   Safari all work).
2. Fill in the fields that apply to your book. Your typed answers and checked
   boxes print along with the page.
3. Choose **File > Print** (or press Ctrl+P / Cmd+P). On-screen controls hide
   automatically and each page fits cleanly on paper.
4. To save a filled copy, choose **"Save as PDF"** in the print dialog.

No internet connection is needed after you download the kit. Nothing is saved
to a server and nothing is tracked — your information stays on your computer.

## Large-print friendly

The HTML pages use at least **18 pt body text**, high-contrast black-on-white,
roomy checkboxes and inputs, and generous spacing so they're easy to read and
fill in by hand. Print styles strip colors and backgrounds to save ink.
