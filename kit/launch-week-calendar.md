# Launch-Week Calendar

A simple day-by-day plan for your first week after publishing on KDP. Fill in
the dates, check off what you do, and add your own tasks in the blank rows.

## Day 0 — Day before launch

- [ ] Manuscript and cover are final; no more changes planned
- [ ] KDP files uploaded and previewed; proof copy on the way (or already reviewed)
- [ ] Launch date and time confirmed
- [ ] Announcement posts written and scheduled
- [ ] Email to your list drafted and scheduled
- [ ] Amazon book link copied and ready to share

## Day 1 — Launch day

- [ ] Hit Publish in KDP
- [ ] Confirm your book is live on Amazon (can take 24–72 hours)
- [ ] Share the launch on social media and your email list
- [ ] Tell friends and family (ask for honest reviews only, never paid ones)
- [ ] Add the book to your website and author bio
- [ ] Take a breath — you published a book

## Day 2 — Day after launch

- [ ] Check that your book page looks right (cover, description, categories)
- [ ] Respond to any messages or comments
- [ ] Post a thank-you to anyone who bought or shared
- [ ] Note first-day sales in KDP dashboard

## Day 3 — Keep momentum

- [ ] Share a review or reader comment (with permission)
- [ ] Post in one relevant group or forum (follow its rules, no spamming)
- [ ] Send a reminder to anyone who missed the launch email
- [ ] Update your email signature or website with the book link

## Day 4 — Promotion

- [ ] Try one paid or free promotion (newsletter feature, ad, price discount)
- [ ] Share a different angle: why you wrote the book, who it's for
- [ ] Track what you spent and any resulting sales

## Day 5 — Midweek check

- [ ] Review KDP sales dashboard and pages read (Kindle Unlimited, if enrolled)
- [ ] Fix any typos or issues readers flagged (re-upload manuscript if needed)
- [ ] Adjust categories or keywords if your book isn't showing in the right searches
- [ ] Plan the weekend post

## Day 6 — Weekend push

- [ ] One more personal share (story behind the book, a reading, a Q&A)
- [ ] Thank reviewers and buyers publicly where appropriate
- [ ] Note weekend sales

## Day 7 — Wrap up

- [ ] Review the week: what sold, what didn't, what feedback you got
- [ ] Write down three things to do differently next launch
- [ ] Schedule a check-in in 30 days to review long-term sales
- [ ] Celebrate again
